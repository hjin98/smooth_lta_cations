# v7.16 molten-salt occlusion: hand-off for a fresh session

This is coordination state, not authority. If this file and the workplan
disagree, the workplan `workplans/V7.16-MOLTEN-SALT-OCCLUSION.md` wins. The
accepted specs under `doc/specs/` win over both.

## Objective

Replace v7.15's sparse cage-centre chloride with a per-cage, variable-loading
amorphous salt phase:

- α-cage: 0-12 Cl⁻ per cage. β-cage: 0-1 Cl⁻ per cage. The Al24 primitive
  cell has 2 α + 2 β cages.
- Each excess cation goes either on a 4R/6R/8R ring site (existing code) or
  into the cage salt phase. Any supported cation or dopant may join the
  LiCl–KCl base salt.
- The generator builds the cage salt on the fly under two kinds of
  constraint:
  - data-sourced pair minimum distances, larger for same-sign pairs;
  - first-neighbour opposite-charge coordination distributions.
- Salt-loading volume contraction is modelled from supplied data, with
  `--volume-factor` kept as an override.
- The v7.15 sparse mode stays as an opt-in legacy mode.
