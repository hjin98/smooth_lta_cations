# v7.16 Variable-loading molten-salt occlusion — D1 intake and change plan

**Status (2026-10-17, second session): D1 draft written, awaiting independent
falsification review and stakeholder ratification.** No accepted D1-D4
authority has changed. The draft is `doc/specs/v7_16_molten_salt_occlusion_extension.md`
(lifecycle PROPOSED). Nothing in this file is accepted scientific authority.

## 0. Resume here

- **Governed objective.** Replace the v7.15 sparse cage-centre chloride model
  with a per-cage, variable-loading amorphous salt phase (α 0-12 Cl, β 0-1 Cl),
  built under data-sourced pair minima and opposite-charge first-shell
  coordination targets, with a modelled salt-loading volume contraction and the
  v7.15 model kept as an explicitly selected legacy mode.
- **Authorities at base.** v7.13 chemistry, v7.14 dry volume, v7.15 sparse
  occlusion (all accepted-current), `doc/archs/lta_cation_generator_architecture.md`.
- **Evidence (non-authoritative, stakeholder-supplied rev 3, 2026-10-17).**
  `doc/evidence/`: occlusion evidence rev 2.1 (replaces the rev 2.0 copy first
  committed), pair-distance table rev 2.1, coordination numbers rev 3.0,
  lattice contraction rev 3.0, plus their CSVs and generator scripts. SHA-256 of
  the three CSVs are in the draft spec §1.
- **Draft spec decisions still open:** draft spec §15, "Open [DECIDE]": the
  dense threshold n_CN and the linear CN–density form, the α-cage charge rule,
  and the contraction form. Review 1 is folded in (spec §17). D-14..D-16 are
  recorded, and P7 (density-reduced CN) is in spec §12.
- **Next action.**
  1. Fold in the independent falsification review.
  2. Present the gate evidence and the §15 decisions to the stakeholder.
  3. On ratification: flip the spec lifecycle, reconcile the v7.15/v7.14/v7.13
     cross-references, then D2 (`numerical-algorithm-design`), D3, D4.

## 3. Evidence digest (from `doc/evidence/lta_salt_occlusion_evidence.md`)

- **Loading.** 11 ± 1 Cl per pseudo-cell for LiCl–KCl–MeCl at 823 K [R1].
  10 ± 1 with Ca/Sr/U [R2]. 12 [R5]. ≈12.9 [R6]. ≈10.2 [R8]. Pure LiCl gives
  10-11.5 but is metastable [R9, R22-R24]. Process loading of 3.4-3.9 is a
  different quantity [R4, R5] (§1).
- **Siting.** Nitrate occlusion is in the α-cage [R12, R26]. Cations are
  members of occluded clusters that fill the pores [R20]. Cl⁻ is anchored to
  6R, 8R or cage-centre cations, never isolated [R27-R30]. The β-cage holds
  one halide by default ([M₄Cl]³⁺, sodalite stoichiometry [R4]). The β-Cl /
  MCl unit therefore stays a training hypothesis (§4, §6.1).
- **Cation dependence.** K⁺ suppresses or blocks occlusion [R7, R11, R13].
  Separation factors vs Li at infinite dilution: Cs 2.31, Sr 11.9, U 13
  [R1, R2]. Cs prefers occlusion sites; Li and Sr prefer exchange sites
  [R14, R16] (§2).
- **Pair-distance data actually present in the file:**
  - Cl–K 2.70 Å, K⁺ bonded to β-Cl through a 6-ring, in a non-melt crystal
    [R27] (§4);
  - α-cage Cl–Cl geometry: 3.22 Å is "too short for simultaneous
    occupation", while 4.04 Å (3-fold sites) and 4.95 Å (4-fold sites) are
    allowed (§5);
  - melt densities ρ (LiCl 1.50, NaCl 1.55, KCl 1.52, CsCl 2.79 g/cm³). The
    file marks these as approximate handbook values that were **not
    retrieved** (§5);
  - α-cage volume ≈ 776 Å³ from an 11.4 Å free diameter [R31] (§5).
- **No data in the file** for ion–framework-O distances, for like-cation
  distances directly, or for Rb, Group-II or trivalent dopant pairs.

## 4. Proposed design after reconsideration (agent proposal, unratified)

### 4.1 Site classes and inventory

- **Ring sites** R ∈ {4, 6, 8}: capacities 24 / 16 / 6 per Al24 cell. These
  hold framework cations (Q_fw = +24, unchanged) and optional ring-placed
  excess cations (the existing `occlusion_counterion` role).
- **Cage regions** Ω_α,k and Ω_β,k, k ∈ {0, 1}. Each holds a per-cage
  inventory: Cl count (α ≤ 12, β ≤ 1) and cage-phase cation counts per species
  (any supported cation, D-7).
- **Charge:** Q_fw = 24 independently. Q_ring-excess + Σ_cages Q_cage = N_Cl
  in total. There is no per-cage neutrality requirement (D-5, D-6).

### 4.2 Pair minimum distances from data (replaces radii and Coulomb)

The proposed rule is d_ij ≥ s_class · b_ij. Here b_ij is a **base distance
taken from a data table**, and s is a scaling factor (the stakeholder's
original "fixed number times").

The default table is derived from the evidence file wherever possible
(Grade D derivation, inputs as graded in the file):

- **Unlike pair M–Cl:** b = d_MX = (v/2)^{1/3}, with v = M/(ρ N_A) from §5
  melt densities. This is the rock-salt-like mean cation–anion spacing.
- **Like pairs:** b = √2 × mean(d_MX) of the two species. This is the
  rock-salt like-ion geometry, so like pairs automatically get a larger
  cut-off (D-3). For Cl–Cl, use the volume-additive mixture of the cage's salt
  composition.

| Pair base | Li | Na | K | Cs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| d_MCl (Å) | 2.863 | 3.152 | 3.440 | 3.687 |
| √2·d_MCl, M–M (Å) | 4.049 | 4.457 | 4.865 | 5.214 |

- Cl–Cl base for the LiCl–KCl eutectic (58.5:41.5) is 4.425 Å.
- Cross-check: the [R27] crystal contact K–Cl 2.70 Å divided by d_KCl 3.440 Å
  gives s ≈ 0.785.
- Cross-check: §5 says Cl–Cl 3.22 Å is forbidden and 4.04 Å allowed. With
  s = 0.75-0.8 the Cl–Cl minimum is 3.3-3.5 Å, which lies inside that bracket.
- **Missing pairs** (Rb, Group II, other dopants, ion–framework O/Si/Al) have
  no evidence-file data. Proposal: a stakeholder-supplied pair table that
  carries a source tag per entry. **A missing pair fails closed** with no
  silent radius fallback (see O3).

### 4.3 Coordination target (D-5, D-8, D-9)

- The stakeholder supplies coordination-number distributions (format: O4).
- The generator aims to reproduce them approximately as a statistical
  distribution, not exactly.
- Candidate D2 method (not decided): random insertion, then penalty relaxation
  to satisfy the hard pair minima (Packmol-type: L. Martínez, R. Andrade,
  E. G. Birgin, J. M. Martínez, *J. Comput. Chem.* 30, 2157-2164 (2009)), then
  a few cheap reverse-Monte-Carlo sweeps pulling the CN histogram toward the
  target (R. L. McGreevy, L. Pusztai, *Mol. Simul.* 1, 359-367 (1988)).
- The run stops at a tolerance or after a sweep budget. All hard minima are
  verified exactly on the final written structure. A run that cannot meet
  them fails closed.
- Low effort is intended (D-9): no claim of uniform sampling or of an
  equilibrium distribution.

### 4.4 Other elements

- **β-cage:** 0/1 Cl, with an optional cage-phase cation (an MCl unit) or a
  ring counterion (D-6).
- **Volume (D-11):**
  - V = V_dry,fw(framework population, v7.14) × g(loading), where g is a
    contraction model interpolated from stakeholder data (form: O5);
  - `--volume-factor` remains an override, with its semantics in O5;
  - the v7.15 statement "framework-only volume" becomes the dry/legacy case.
- **Legacy (D-10):** the v7.15 fixed-centre sparse path is kept, reachable
  only through an explicit legacy selector (e.g. `--occlusion-model
  sparse-v715`), and must stay output-identical for the same seed. The new
  per-cage inputs get new flag names so `--alpha-cl/--beta-cl` keep their
  v7.15 per-cell meaning (O7).
- **Ring cations during the salt build (D-12):** a ring cation that blocks
  the cage salt may move only to an empty site of its own ring family;
  otherwise it stays. Ring-family counts never change, and cut-offs against
  ring cations are the same as for any other pair.
- **Coordination (D-13):** CN is counted only over first-neighbour
  opposite-charge pairs (cation–Cl, Cl–cation).
- **Pipeline order:**
  1. ring placement;
  2. density smoothing (ring cations only);
  3. cage-salt build against the ring geometry, with D-12 same-family
     relocation when needed;
  4. final constraint check;
  5. volume model;
  6. write.
- **Unchanged:** Al24-only scope, canonical template gating, rejection of
  maximum-symmetry and unique-batch modes when occlusion is active.

## 5. Agent probe results (exploratory, non-production, not acceptance evidence)

The probe scripts are in `workplans/v716-probes/`. All runs used the same
setup: fixture `tests/real_lta_primitive.cif`; generator seed 0; ring
population Na₂₄ fixed (4R: 2, 6R: 16, 8R: 6); both α-cages loaded with n
LiCl–KCl pairs (Li:K = 58.5:41.5, rounded); cage-phase ions confined to 6.0 Å
of the α centre; framework atoms checked with a Shannon-radius rule
(f = 0.85; Si 0.26, Al 0.39, O 1.40 Å); 12 random starts per point;
L-BFGS-B penalty minimization; "feasible" means penalty < 1e-6.

**Measured geometry** (Na₂₄ cell, V = 3703 Å³):
- α centre → nearest O ≈ 7.0 Å; 4R / 8R / 6R sites at 5.0 / 6.27 / 6.4 Å.
- β centre → nearest O ≈ 4.64 Å; 6R sites at 4.24 Å.

| Probe | Rule | Result |
|---|---|---|
| P1-P3 (superseded by D-3) | same-sign Coulomb threshold d₀, unlike f·(r+r) | 10-12 pairs/α feasible for d₀ ≤ 3.5 Å, infeasible at 3.75 Å and above. β: one LiCl or KCl per β is feasible at d₀ ≤ 3.5 Å. |
| P4 | §4.2 data-derived bases × s, applied to all pairs incl. ring cations | s = 0.70-0.75: feasible at 8-12/α (6-8 of 12 starts). s = 0.80: 10-12/α feasible but rare (1-2 of 12). s = 0.85: feasible only up to 10/α (1 of 12). s ≥ 0.90: infeasible even at 8/α. |
| P4, ring relaxed | like-ion minima vs ring cations scaled by s_ring = 0.70 | s = 0.85-0.90 feasible at 10/α. 12/α feasible at s ≤ 0.90. s = 1.0 feasible at 10/α but not at 12/α. |

- **Variant disclosure.** About 90 grid points were evaluated:
  - P1: f ∈ {0.8, 0.9}, d₀ ∈ {3.0, 3.5, 4.0}, n ∈ {6, 8, 10, 11, 12};
  - P2/P3: d₀ ∈ {3.25, 3.5, 3.6, 3.75, 4.0}, n ∈ {1, 2, 10, 11}, plus four
    β-cage cases;
  - P4: s ∈ {0.70, 0.75, 0.80, 0.85, 0.90, 1.0} × n ∈ {8, 10, 11, 12}, plus
    six ring-relaxed points.

  All used one ring arrangement and one seed. Nothing was selected as a
  default, and every grid point is reported.
- **Not examined:**
  - Li-A, K-A or mixed ring populations;
  - other seeds and ring arrangements;
  - dopants;
  - coordination targets;
  - whether the configurations look realistic or are evenly spread;
  - alternative cage-region definitions;
  - ion–O cut-offs from real data.
- **Claim limits.** "Feasible" means a configuration was actually built.
  "0 of 12" is not a proof of infeasibility. Every parameter was chosen by the
  agent.

## 5a. Findings and tensions (agent-asserted, Claude Code session 2026-10-17)

- **T1 (material).** Evidence §5 says 10 Cl per cell is about 70-80 % of bulk
  melt packing. That estimate omits the 12 framework cations per pseudo-cell
  that line the α-cage. P4 shows that bulk-melt-like spacings (s ≥ 0.9) are
  infeasible at ≥ 8 pairs/α while ring cations are held fixed. Either the
  cut-offs must be compressed (s ≈ 0.75-0.8, consistent with the [R27]
  ratio 0.785) or ring cations must be allowed closer or allowed to move (O1).
  Revisit when the stakeholder's pair and coordination data arrive.
- **T2.** The §5 melt densities were not retrieved from a source, so every
  derived base distance inherits that weakness. Bind sourced densities before
  D1 ratification.
- **T3.** The evidence file has no data for ion–framework distances or for
  dopant pairs, but D-3 requires data-sourced cut-offs. A stakeholder table is
  needed; otherwise those pairs fail closed.
- **T4 (minor, citation).** The v7.15 spec cites "Lewis, Fischer, Smith" and
  "Simpson and Gougar". The evidence file marks both author lists
  "to confirm" (R10, R14). Reconcile in the v7.16 D1 references.
- **T5.** Trivalent dopants (U³⁺, lanthanides) are in the evidence [R2] but
  outside the v7.13 Group-I/II chemistry authority (O6).

## 5b. Second-session findings (agent-asserted, Claude Code session https://claude.ai/code/session_01Cu3UQGuLVrywXY9rpJEEJi, 2026-10-17)

These supersede §4.2 (rock-salt bases and the scaling factor s) and update §5a.
The full record is in the draft spec §12 (probes) and §13 (tension
assessments). Raw results are in `workplans/v716-probes/results/p5_all.jsonl`
and `p6_cn.jsonl`, produced by `probe_p5_supplied_cutoffs_d12.py`.

- **P5 (132 points).** With the supplied r_cut column, the D-12 relocation and
  F2 framework proxy, 8-12 Cl/α are feasible for Na/K/Li-A rings at every
  point (24/24 starts with relocation; 19-23/24 without). The result is
  insensitive to the framework proxy, cage-region definition, β mode and
  f_V = 0.97. Cs/Sr/Ba dopants are feasible. A Ca ring is rejected for the
  missing (Ca, Cl) pair. T1 is inapplicable under these cut-offs.
- **Unshaped coordination is far below target.** Li–Cl ≈ 2 against 4.3, and
  some ions have no opposite-charge first neighbour. CN shaping is needed, and
  the draft adds a hard anchoring rule.
- **P6 (36 points, smooth CN shaping).** Both candidate target sets are reached
  in the confined cage with all hard minima met. Cl-centred counts including
  ring cations come out at 5.4-7.1, above bulk (new tension T7).
- **Still missing from the evidence:**
  - ion–framework minimum distances (pair file §6.4 defers to framework-O
    values it does not supply);
  - Mg/Ca/H/Be/Fr/Ra pair rows;
  - any loading dependence of the contraction.
- **v7.15 reopen triggers.** A third one now fires: "dry volume + explicit
  factor" is unsuitable, because salt-loaded 4A contracts [R18].

## 6. Open questions for the stakeholder

- **O1: resolved by D-12.** Two follow-ups remain:
  - **O1a.** Does moving to another positional variant of the *same*
    physical site (e.g. the 4 coordinate variants of one 8R window) count as an
    allowed same-family move? Proposed: yes.
  - **O1b.** Do both framework and ring-placed excess cations move? Proposed:
    yes.

  Feasibility under D-12 is **not probed yet**. P4's "ring relaxed" rows used
  a reduced cut-off, which D-12 does not adopt. With all 16 6R sites occupied
  in Na-A, 6R ions cannot move at all, so the s window may be close to the
  fixed-ring P4 rows (s ≈ 0.75-0.8 for 10-12/α).
- **O2.** Scaling factor s: one value for all pairs, or separate values per
  class (unlike, like, against ring cations, against framework)? Default?
  The data cross-checks point to about 0.75-0.8.
- **O3.** The pair-cut-off table: will you supply ion–framework-O and dopant
  pairs? Is the rock-salt derivation in §4.2 acceptable for Li/Na/K/Cs pairs?
  Should missing pairs fail closed (proposed)?
- **O4: partly resolved by D-13** (first nearest neighbours, opposite charge
  only). Still needed with the data:
  - **O4a.** The first-shell cut-off radius per opposite-charge pair (e.g. the
    first minimum of g(r)), from the supplied data.
  - **O4b.** Distribution form: a histogram P(CN) per central species, or a
    mean ± spread.
  - **O4c.** Do ring-site cations count as first neighbours of cage-phase Cl?
    Proposed reading of D-13: yes, since they are opposite-charge ions and §4
    says Cl is anchored to 6R/8R cations. To be confirmed.
- **O5.** Contraction data format:
  - relative ΔV/V (or lattice parameter) versus Cl per pseudo-cell, and
    whether it depends on composition;
  - interpolation type;
  - whether `--volume-factor` **replaces** the modelled volume or
    **multiplies** it.
- **O6.** Are trivalent dopants (U³⁺, lanthanides) in scope? That would
  reopen the v7.13 chemistry authority.
- **O7.** Flag names: confirm new per-cage inputs (e.g. `--alpha-salt
  Cl:10,Li:6,K:4`, per-cage overrides, an optional JSON spec file) and keep
  `--alpha-cl/--beta-cl` legacy-only.
- **O8.** Cage-region definition: a sphere set by the [R31] 11.4 Å free
  diameter, or a nearest-cage-centre partition (which lets ions sit in 8R
  windows)?

## 9. Reopen / stop triggers

- Stakeholder data contradicts the §4.2 rock-salt derivation or the s window.
- Feasibility at 12/α cannot be achieved under the ratified cut-offs together
  with the D-12 same-family relocation policy. If so, raise it to the
  stakeholder; do not silently lower the cap or reduce cut-offs against ring
  cations.
- Trivalent dopants are required (O6 → v7.13 reopen).
- The contraction model needs composition dependence beyond the supplied
  data.
