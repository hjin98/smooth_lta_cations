LTA cation generator v7.14 validation record
=============================================

Scope
-----
This historical v7.14 validation record predates the v7.14.1 provenance
clarification.  v7.14 replaces v7.13's whole-cell input-volume fallback for
non-Li/Na/K compositions with a per-cation affine dry-volume model.  Where no
species-specific dry-LTA volume reference is bound by the package, that species
is bound to the standard dehydrated Na-A volume as an explicit placeholder.
This wording describes the package's bound references and is not an exhaustive
literature-absence claim.

Volume contract
---------------
For cation species i with formal charge z_i and population N_i,

    V_ref = (1/24) * sum_i[z_i * N_i * V_i^(24)]

and

    V_target = volume_factor * V_ref.

Charge neutrality supplies sum_i(z_i*N_i) = N_Al.  The formula is therefore
exactly the historical count/24 interpolation for Li/Na/K-only compositions
and uses charge-equivalent fractions when +2 cations are present.

Source caveats
--------------
- Mg: the source reports a small residual water content despite vigorous
  dehydration, so the Mg corner is lower-confidence than the dry alkali/Ca data.
- Sr: the 1982 Pluth/Smith redetermination reports approximately 5.7 Sr per
  12-Al pseudocell and an extra-framework Al complex.  The stored value is
  therefore a QUESTIONABLE effective corner, not a clean ideal Sr6-A endpoint.
- Ba: fully Ba-exchanged LTA is reported unstable toward dehydration.  The Ba
  value is therefore an affine model corner extrapolated from fully dehydrated
  Ba3.5Na5-A, not a claim that stable dry Ba6-A exists.
- H, Be, Fr, Ra: no species-specific dry-LTA volume reference is bound by the
  package for these species; each uses the Na-A corner by contract.  This is
  not an exhaustive claim about all literature.
