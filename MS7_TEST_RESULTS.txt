MS7 test and benchmark report
=============================

Release: v7.12 MS7
Date: 2026-07-23

Implemented accelerations
-------------------------
- Expansion-provenance framework actions with vectorized geometric fallback.
- Generator-orbit closure for symmetry-completed variants.
- Integer-bit-mask subgroup closure and exhaustive lattice enumeration.
- Exact direct raw-occupation enumeration below a configurable threshold.
- Reuse of existing DensityReport objects during smoothing score evaluation.
- Public strategy/work diagnostics in validation and optimality reports.

Correctness tests
-----------------

- accelerated construction diagnostics;
- full-group closure of the completed variant domain;
- direct versus subgroup-orbit occupation-solver equivalence;
- bit-mask closure on a known cyclic group;
- density-report score reuse;
- the real 48-operation primitive Na-LTA case.

Environment limitation
----------------------
The execution image did not contain installed pymatgen or spglib and had no
network access. Regression tests and timings therefore used an API-compatible
local harness located outside the release archive. The release itself retains
its normal numpy/pymatgen dependencies. The algorithmic equivalence tests and
relative hotspot reductions are valid; absolute wall times should be repeated
in the production pymatgen environment.
